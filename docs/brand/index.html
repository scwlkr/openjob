01 / Identity

An open frame.
A connected workspace.

Two offset planes form one clear opening. Cobalt folds give the mark its character; the center stays open.

Space to breathe. Leave at least one ribbon width around the mark. Give the lockup half the mark’s height in clear space.

Keep its shape. Preserve the open center, fold direction, and proportions. Use the lockup at 120 px wide or above. Use the mark alone below that.

Write OpenJob. Capital O, capital J. No period. The mark stands on its own; keep extra effects out of the logo.

02 / Color

A cool, clear foundation.

Chalk gives the work room. Graphite carries the words. Cobalt identifies the next action. Blue Gray and Sage support selection, accounts, and calendar blocks.

Pair color with purpose.

Keep most of the workspace neutral. Reserve filled Cobalt for the main action. Add labels or symbols alongside account colors and permission states.

Say what happens.
